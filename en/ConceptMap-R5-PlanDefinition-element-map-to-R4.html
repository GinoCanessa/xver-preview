The intent of the timing element is to provide timing when the action should be performed. PlanDefinition.versionAlgorithm[x] (versionAlgorithm[x])​maps to wider concepthttp://hl7.org/fhir/5.0/StructureDefinition/extension-PlanDefinition.versionAlgorithmElement `PlanDefinition.versionAlgorithm[x]` has a context of PlanDefinition based on following the parent source element upwards and mapping to `PlanDefinition`.
