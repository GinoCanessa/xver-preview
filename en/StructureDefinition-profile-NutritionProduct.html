Resource Profile: Profile_R5_NutritionProduct_R4

Official URL: http://hl7.org/fhir/5.0/StructureDefinition/profile-NutritionProduct Version: 0.1.0
Standards status: Trial-use Active as of 2026-03-04 Maturity Level: 0 Computable Name: Profile_R5_NutritionProduct_R4

This cross-version profile allows R5 NutritionProduct content to be represented via FHIR R4 Basic resources.

Usages:

  • Refer to this Profile: R5: Information about use of the product in relation to other therapies described as part of the contraindication (new), R5: Specifics for when this is a contraindication (new), R5: Specifics for when this is an interaction (new), R5: Association with other items or products (new)... Show 9 more, R5: Link to a product resource used in clinical workflows (new), R5: An inventory listing section (grouped by any of the attributes) (new), R5: What food or fluid product or item was consumed (new), R5: type additional types, R5: baseFormulaType additional types, R5: type additional types, R5: Ingredients contained in this product (new), R5: A packaging item, as a container for medically related items, possibly with other packaging items within, or a packaging component, such as bottle cap (new) and R5: item additional types
