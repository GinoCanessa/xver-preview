Resource Profile: Profile_R5_PackagedProductDefinition_R4

Official URL: http://hl7.org/fhir/5.0/StructureDefinition/profile-PackagedProductDefinition Version: 0.1.0
Standards status: Trial-use Active as of 2026-03-04 Maturity Level: 0 Computable Name: Profile_R5_PackagedProductDefinition_R4

This cross-version profile allows R5 PackagedProductDefinition content to be represented via FHIR R4 Basic resources.

Usages:

  • Refer to this Profile: R5: The packaged medication that is being priced (new) and R5: A packaging item, as a container for medically related items, possibly with other packaging items within, or a packaging component, such as bottle cap (new)
